How I would use this in a team
  1. Agree the definition of done before work starts.
  2. Assign one accountable owner and a realistic due date.
  3. Update progress and dependencies at agreed check-in points.
  4. Escalate at-risk work with a clear decision or support request.
  5. Close completed tasks and capture any handover actions.
